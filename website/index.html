Why Choose Us

The Grace Found Difference

When choosing a home care provider, it's important to know whether the agency directly employs its caregivers. At Grace Found Home Care Agency LLC, we do.

Direct Employment

All caregivers are our employees, not independent contractors, ensuring a safe, professional, and accountable care experience.

No Money Exchange

There is no exchange of money between clients and caregivers. We handle all payroll and tax responsibilities.

Full Insurance Coverage

Our caregivers are covered by workers' compensation and liability insurance for your complete peace of mind.

RN Supervision

All clients and caregivers are overseen by a Registered Nurse who conducts in-home assessments and creates customized care plans.

Affordable Rates

Competitive hourly and live-in rates. We proudly serve individuals on fixed incomes with exceptional, dependable care.
